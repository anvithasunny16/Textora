Frequently Asked Questions
Can AI detection guarantee 100% accuracy? ▼
No. Current artificial intelligence text detectors cannot establish authorship with absolute certainty. Both false positives (flagging human writing as synthetic) and false negatives (missing generated text) are common. Textora AI provides transparent statistical diagnostics so you can inspect the exact linguistic reasons behind any score.
What is "Burstiness" and why does it matter? ▼
Burstiness measures the variation in sentence length and syntactic complexity. Human authors naturally alternate between short, punchy statements and long, descriptive clauses. Large language models (LLMs) tend to produce sentences with remarkably uniform length (typically 16–22 words per sentence), resulting in low burstiness.
Why are short texts flagged with "Low Confidence"? ▼
Linguistic metrics like Type-Token Ratio, Hapax Legomena, and sentence-length variance require a statistically meaningful sample size. Passages under 50 words lack sufficient data points to draw reliable conclusions, which is why Textora AI clearly indicates low confidence.
Are scanned PDF files supported? ▼
Textora AI automatically checks whether a PDF contains an embedded text layer. If a scanned document or image-only PDF is uploaded, the platform detects the absence of text and displays a clear notice explaining that Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is required.
